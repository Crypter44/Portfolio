01Robotum: Advanced Project

Project Hestia

An autonomous, self-operating kitchen

The long-term idea behind Hestia is a kitchen that cooks, cleans and runs itself, but still lets you cook by hand whenever you feel like it. It's an advanced project at Robotum and still early days. 02Team Project: ROS2

A drone that explores caves on its own

A ROS2 software stack for a UAV that explores and maps an unknown cave system on its own, finds landmarks and records where they are, with nobody at the controls. 03Personal Project: Android

Reversi

A Minimax AI you can actually lose to

A native Android version of the classic board game Reversi (Othello). The fun part is the opponent: Minimax with alpha-beta pruning, plus several board-evaluation heuristics that I pitted against each other to see which plays strongest at which search depth.

05Personal Project: Simulation

Game Physics

Fake it till it looks real

Three physics simulations in Python, written with NumPy and SciPy and rendered with Matplotlib. They're not meant to be physically exact, the goal is something that looks right and is cheap enough to run in real time. There's a heated plate (heat spreading from a single hotspot, solved with finite differences, forward and implicit Euler), a cloth (a grid of masses joined by structural, shear and flexion springs, integrated with RK2 or implicit Euler) and a fluid (vortices evolving under the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations, with semi-Lagrangian advection and a pressure projection on a staggered grid).

Python NumPy / SciPy Finite Differences Spring-Mass Systems Navier-Stokes
